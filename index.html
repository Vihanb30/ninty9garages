Who we are

Care for your car, done properly.

Ninty9 Garages is a detailing and protection studio in Gwalior. Whether it's a daily hatchback, a family SUV or a trail-ready Thar, we clean, correct, protect and upgrade it with the same attention to detail.

We're an authorised studio for the brands we fit (Aozoom, Bimbra, Turtle Wax and Nshield), so the parts are genuine and come with the manufacturer's warranty where it applies. Every job is planned around your vehicle, with no generic packages. Just the right work for your build.
